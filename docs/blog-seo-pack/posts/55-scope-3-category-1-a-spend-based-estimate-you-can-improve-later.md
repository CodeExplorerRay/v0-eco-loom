# Scope 3 Category 1: A Spend-Based Estimate You Can Improve Later

Scope 3 Accounting | December 19, 2026 | 4 min read

**Topic:** Scope 3 Accounting
**Primary Keyword:** spend-based Category 1 emissions estimate
**Publish Date:** December 19, 2026
**Status:** Ready to Publish

A spend-based estimate can help a team create an initial view of purchased goods and services when supplier or physical activity data is not yet available. It is not a shortcut to certainty. The result depends on how purchasing data is classified, which factors are applied, and whether the spend represents the activity the team intends to measure.

Treat the estimate as a starting point with an explicit improvement path.

## Prepare the purchasing data

Define the reporting period, currency basis, and treatment of taxes, credits, and refunds according to the chosen method. Remove duplicates and map vendor names to stable IDs. Reconcile the cleaned total to finance records so transformations do not silently change the scale of the estimate.

Keep unusual items and capital purchases visible for separate review rather than forcing every transaction into a generic category.

## Build a category mapping that can be maintained

Map purchases to categories using descriptions, item codes, and procurement knowledge. Use a documented rule for ambiguous transactions and record the confidence of the mapping. Ask category owners to review high-value or high-impact groups.

A mapping table should include effective dates and a change log. This makes it possible to explain why a category total changes even when purchasing activity did not.

## Apply factors with clear limits

Choose factors that align with the category, currency year, geography, and method. Record the factor source and unit conversions. A spend factor estimates emissions from economic value; it does not reveal the actual material quantity or supplier process.

Explain this limitation in the inventory notes and avoid comparing the estimate directly with a supplier-specific result without checking comparability.

## Plan the next data upgrade

Use the results to identify where better data could matter most. A focused supplier request or purchase-quantity export may improve a priority category more than trying to replace every proxy at once. Track the method and coverage by category over time.

**Key Takeaway:** A spend-based Category 1 estimate is most useful when its purchasing inputs and factors are documented and each major proxy has a path to improvement.
